# `site_*` models and `w*_terrain` meshes

* **`site_*`** are converted parts of the *LowPoly House Construction Site* pack by **Majadroid** (Maik Hoffmann),
  published under **CC0** (public domain; attribution not required but appreciated). Colour palette by Imphenzia.
  Source: https://majadroid.itch.io/ (also https://opengameart.org/users/majadroid).
  They were converted from FBX to OBJ + MTL + palette PNG by `src/nav/scripts/build_world.py`
  (reader: `src/nav/scripts/worldgen/fbx.py`). Do not edit by hand - rebuild with the build script.
* **`w3_excavation_slopes_terrain`** is generated terrain (our own, procedural).
